## Target audience
My target audience is policymakers or people who are able to call to action in a way that draws attention, and who are also able to take tangible steps from there on. Since it is difficult to get into contact with policymakers, I interviewed fellow classmates at the policy school-- a second year MISM student and a second year PPM-DA. 

## Interview script
1. Is it clear that these are ratios?
2. Is the flow or the connection between the visualizations clear?
3. If I didn't explain or provide as much context, would you get the same level of understanding just by looking at these visualizations?
4. Is the gap between men and women clear enough? If not, what can I do?

## Interview findings
Feedback:
1. The fact that they are ratios is not very clear, write this instead of share in the axes or include it in titles.
2. Simplify titles, they are a little hard to follow and you can still make your point in a more concise way.
3. A good idea is to include annotations that call more attention to the gap-- including ones where you state what the gap % is.
4. Some color changes could be a good idea, such as less use of gray.
